Investment Hub

Insurance investment portfolios: administration, control and accounting

Instruments

The single register of financial instruments, shared by every portfolio and legal entity. Positions, book value and fair value are calculated from the lots, never typed in.

Instrument

Static data, cash-flow schedule, positions by portfolio and lot, prices and history of one instrument. Every figure opens its calculation.

Portfolios

Portfolios and sub-portfolios of the legal entity by allocation type, with the coverage of technical provisions by the allocated assets.

Positions & lots

Positions by portfolio and instrument at the reference date. Expand a position to see its lots, each with its own cost, effective interest rate and IFRS 9 classification.

Asset allocation

Allocate, reallocate and release assets between portfolios. Nothing is overwritten: every movement keeps origin, destination, who recorded and who approved.

Transactions

Purchases, sales, coupons, redemptions and other movements. Coupons and redemptions are proposed from the cash-flow schedule and confirmed against the custodian.

Valuations

Prices by date and source with the IFRS 13 level. Missing, stale or unusual prices are held as exceptions, and manual prices need approval.

Impairment (ECL)

Expected credit losses by lot under IFRS 9: stage, probability of default, loss given default and exposure, with the movement of the period.

Investment limits

Use of internal and regulatory limits by issuer, group, country, rating and asset class. Breaches need a justification and an owner.

Period close & entries

Guided monthly close and the journal entries generated by the accounting rules, ready to export to the ERP.

Reports

Inventory, allocation and coverage map, Solvency II list of assets (S.06.02), income and gains (S.09.01) and movement schedules. Every report can be reproduced at a past closing date.

Approvals

Everything that needs a second pair of eyes: transactions, reallocations, manual prices, impairment overrides and parameter changes. Whoever recorded cannot approve.

Audit trail

Every change to every record: previous value, new value, who, when, why and from where.

Backoffice

Parameters that change the behaviour of the platform without code: accounting rules, chart of accounts, portfolio types and dimensions, PD and LGD tables, price tolerances and limits.
